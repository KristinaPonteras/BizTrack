Important:
- Product names and brands are real retail items.
- Selling prices and quantities are intentionally blank. Enter the actual price and on-hand quantity from the store or a verified supplier/retailer; do not guess.
- Package sizes and product variants can differ by retailer and location, so verify the exact variant before creating the live record.
- This CSV is a data-entry guide. It does not automatically insert records into the live database.
- Add categories first, then add products through StoreFlow's Products page, or import the CSV only if your app has a supported import feature.
